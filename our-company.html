What we are, and what we are not.

Wealth management has many kinds of firm in it. It helps to know exactly which one you are dealing with.

We are

  • A software companyWe design, build and look after the planning tool your advisers use.
  • Accountable for the softwareIts accuracy, its security and its upkeep are ours to answer for.
  • Your supplier, and nobody else's agentWe work for the firms that license us, not for anyone whose products might appear in a plan.

We are not

  • A bank or a custodianWe never hold client money or securities.
  • An asset managerWe manage no one's money and run no portfolios.
  • A financial adviserWe never advise your clients. Your advisers do.

Our mission

Make a real financial plan the normal starting point for advice, not a luxury kept for the largest clients.

Our vision

Every household that takes advice also holds a plan it understands, and can see how each decision changes it.

How we got here

From a planner in a browser to a platform for whole firms.

  1. The beginning

    It started in the browser.

    The first Wealth Analyzer ran entirely inside an adviser's web browser: no server, no account, and nothing stored anywhere but the adviser's own machine. It proved that serious planning did not need a heavy system around it.

  2. The rebuild

    Then firms needed more.

    A firm needs more than one person's tool: shared plans, permissions and checks on every record that is saved. So we rebuilt Wealth Analyzer as a platform for whole firms, and re-engineered the planning engine instead of copying it across.

  3. Today

    Live, and in conversation.

    The platform is live, and we are in conversation with private banks, wealth managers and family offices, in Switzerland first and beyond it.

How we are paid

One source of income: the licence.

That is the whole business model. The only way we do well is by building software that is worth paying for, year after year.

No commissions or retrocessionsWe earn nothing from the funds, mandates or products that appear in a plan.
No referral feesNo bank, platform or provider pays us to be recommended, or to recommend them.
Independently ownedNo bank, asset manager or product provider is among our owners.
