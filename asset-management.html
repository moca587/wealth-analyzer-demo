The gap

Your clients do not buy a track record. They buy an answer.

Performance tables answer the question the industry asks. Wealth Analyzer answers the one the client asks, using your strategy as the input.

What your factsheet says

  • Returns over one, three and five years
  • Volatility and maximum drawdown
  • Ongoing charges
  • Performance against a benchmark

  • Will we be all right?
  • Does this mandate make our goals more likely?
  • What does it cost us over thirty years?
  • How bad could a bad decade be?

From strategy to client plan

Four steps from your model portfolio to a decision the client understands.

STEP 01

Your assumptions

Every simulation runs on expected return and volatility by asset class, with correlations between them.

  • Set an expected return for each proposed position
  • Load your research for the AI to weigh
  • Assumptions named in every report

STEP 02

A mix, drafted

The asset mix follows the household's risk profile and horizon. The AI weighs a built-in research library plus anything you load.

  • Tilts the mix by at most 8 points
  • Fund choices follow Swiss, UCITS, US or Canadian rules

STEP 03

Tested in the client's plan

The draft runs inside the household's own plan, with their pensions, property, loans and taxes.

  • How each goal's probability moves
  • What costs take over the full horizon
  • Six stress scenarios on the new mix

STEP 04

Staged, with checks

Once approved, the positions leave as a ticket marked to stage, never to trade, in every format it is sent in.

  • Checked line by line before it leaves
  • Sent once, however often it is retried
  • Refused if it names another account

The range, not a single number

Show the floor, the target and the upside.

Each outcome comes with its odds: the floor is reached in nine simulated futures out of ten, the target in six, the upside in four. That is the conversation a factsheet cannot have: what the strategy makes possible, and how sure the client can be of it.

Order staging

From an approved proposal to a ticket your desk can trust.

The positions go to your portfolio or order management system as a ticket that waits for approval there. Before it leaves, every line is checked.

  • ISIN check digits verified on every lineA ticket with any line lacking both an ISIN and a ticker is held back whole
  • Currency must match the custody accountA mismatch is refused, never quietly converted
  • A ceiling on ticket size, set by your firmKept on the server, where a browser cannot raise it
  • The custody account comes from the connectionA ticket naming a different account is refused, not rebooked
  • A retry returns the first resultThe same ticket is never placed twice, and a corrected ticket is refused rather than treated as a retry
  • Sent only to hosts you have approvedA list your administrators keep, required in production
